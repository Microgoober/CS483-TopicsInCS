Accessibility:
Most of the consideration that was thought of was things we discussed in class. To begin with the accessibility, I added labels to all of my elements, so that way screen readers could access and read them. I also included metadata like the viewports, and the language definition.
  
SEO:
For the video I did add alt text so that they have a rough idea of what's there. For the SEO standards I structure the HTML files as is standard, including title declarations for search engines. I also used the header levels as standard, though I'm not sure more than one paragraph was needed.

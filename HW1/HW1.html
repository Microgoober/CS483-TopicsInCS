Welcome to WSU Vancouver

At WSU Vancouver, we are hidden gems surrounded by nature, with stunning mountain views and lush greenery. And the squirrels? They are always hanging around, like they know the best study spots!

Favorite Campus Landmarks

  • The Cougar Pride Sculpture – A symbol of our school spirit and pride!
  • The Walking Bell – An iconic piece of campus history, whose solemn tones mark the passage of time.
  • The Peace Pole – A reminder that no matter where we come from, we all share the same desire for a peaceful world.
